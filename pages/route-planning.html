About this screen

  • Each customer has a visit frequency (weekly, every two weeks or monthly) and a monthly goal; the expected volume of a day is the goal spread over that customer's visits.
  • Pre-sale and delivery visits are planned separately because they are done by different people on different days.
  • The copy tool previews what will be replaced before anything is written, and can fill only empty days.
  • Compliance compares the plan with what really happened; sales outside the plan are listed apart so they do not hide missed visits.
