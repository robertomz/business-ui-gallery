Rules used (sample rates)

        

          

            
              
Social securityEmployee 3.5%, employer 7%, on gross up to a ceiling of 11,000.00 per period

              
Pension fundEmployee 4%, employer 4%, only on the part of gross above a floor of 6,850.00

              
Housing fundEmployer 1.5% of gross

              
Income taxGross × 24 minus 40,000.00 exempt; 0% to 200k, 15% to 500k, 20% to 1M, 25% above; divided by 24

              
OvertimeHourly rate (monthly salary ÷ 240) × 1.5

            
          

          
These rates are invented for the demo. Real payroll rules depend on each country.

        

      

    


    

      
About this screen

      
    
        
  • Payroll is built in steps: attendance sets the days worked, then earnings, then deductions, and nothing is posted until it is reviewed and applied.
    • 
        
  • Contributions with a ceiling or a floor are calculated per employee, which is where most payroll mistakes happen.
    • 
        
  • Manual bonuses and deductions need a reason and are kept per employee.
    • 
        
  • Applying creates the bank transfer file and one accounting entry: salaries by cost center, withholdings payable and net pay payable, one line per account.
    • 
        
  • People who are active but not in the run are listed with the reason, so nobody is left out by accident.
