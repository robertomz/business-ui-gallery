About this screen

  • Each entry is checked for balance; unbalanced ones are flagged with an icon and text, and can be filtered on their own.
  • "Source document" opens the document that generated the entry, and its layout changes with the source: an invoice, a payroll summary, a bank movement.
  • Posted entries are locked. Editing one needs an approval code that expires after five minutes.
