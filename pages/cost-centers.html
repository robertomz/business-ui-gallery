About this screen

  • Budgets are kept per center, account and month. The projection is the actual to date plus the budget still to come.
  • Nobody edits a budget directly: a change is requested with a reason and becomes effective only after it is approved. Rejecting also needs a reason, so there is always a record.
  • Expenses are split into direct (charged to the center), indirect and shared (allocated from support centers).
  • A new center can copy the expense accounts of a similar one and scale its budget, which saves typing a hundred lines by hand.
