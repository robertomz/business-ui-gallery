About this screen

  • The available balance updates as invoices are selected, and the payment cannot be saved if it leaves the account below zero.
  • Check numbers are suggested from the last one used in that account and must be unique.
  • When a USD account pays invoices in local currency, the amount is converted at the day's rate and the cents that do not convert exactly go to an exchange difference line, so the entry always balances.
  • The entry is shown before saving. Lines for the same account and side are added into one.
