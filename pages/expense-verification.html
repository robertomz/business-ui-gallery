About this screen

  • The real screen stores the scanned receipt and reads it; here the reading is simulated from the file you pick, only its name is used.
  • Supplier names never match exactly ("Northwind Foods Inc." vs "NORTHWIND FOODS"), so the check compares words and gives a similarity score instead of a yes or no.
  • A line is Verified when supplier ≥ 80%, the amount matches to the cent and the date is within 3 days. Small gaps go to Needs review; big ones are a Mismatch.
  • The verification is informative: it does not change the accounting, it tells each center how much of its spending is backed by a receipt.
