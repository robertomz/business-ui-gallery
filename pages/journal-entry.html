About this screen

  • Each line is either a debit or a credit, never both: typing in one column clears the other.
  • Expense accounts (code 6) need a cost center that has budget for that account. Centers without budget are shown but disabled, with the reason.
  • Totals update while typing, and Save stays disabled until the entry balances and every line is valid. Errors appear next to the line that has them.
  • In production this saves through a stored procedure that numbers the entry and posts it in one transaction.
