About this screen

  • Each priority has a response target: Critical 4 hours, High 8, Medium 24 and Low 72. The timer counts live and turns into "breached" when it runs out.
  • Cards can be dragged, but every move also has a button, so the board works with a keyboard and on a phone.
  • Every status change is written to the ticket's timeline, so the history explains how long each step took.
