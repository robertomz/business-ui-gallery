About this screen

  • Each invoice falls in one bucket by its days past due, so the five buckets always add up to the balance shown.
  • The account statement lists invoices and payments in date order with a running balance that ends at the same number as the aging table.
  • Collection effectiveness counts how many invoices were paid within 20 and 40 days, which shows collection habits better than the balance alone.
