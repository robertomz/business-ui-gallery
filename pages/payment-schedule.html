About this screen

  • Due date = issue date + the supplier's credit days. Overdue invoices show an icon and the days late, not only a color.
  • Each invoice is paid from an account in its own currency; USD amounts are shown in local currency with the day's exchange rate.
  • The list cannot be approved if any bank account would go below zero. Approving numbers the list, removes the invoices from the open list and keeps it in the history with a printable view.
