About this screen

  • The weights of all KPIs must add up to exactly 100%; the screen says how far off it is and blocks approval until it does.
  • Each KPI is compared with its cumulative target to date, not with the annual one, so October is not judged against December.
  • KPIs where lower is better (costs, days, complaints) invert the result. The result is capped at 120% so one KPI cannot hide the others.
  • Draft can be edited; Approved freezes targets and weights; Closed freezes everything. Every state change would be logged in the real system.
