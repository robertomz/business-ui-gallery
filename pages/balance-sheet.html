About this screen

  • The tree goes from level 1 (class) to level 5 (subaccount), and every parent adds up its children, so any number can be traced down.
  • The banner checks the accounting equation: assets must equal liabilities plus equity.
  • Until the month is closed, the period result (revenue minus costs and expenses) still lives in the income accounts. Turn off "Include period result" and the banner shows that exact difference.
  • Rows expand with a mouse or the keyboard; the toggle buttons announce whether they are open.
