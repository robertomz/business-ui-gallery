About this screen

  • A scheduled job closes each day at night and stores a snapshot, so any past close opens instantly and is never recalculated.
  • Every card compares against the previous working day and the same day last month. Non-working days are skipped so a Monday is not compared with a Sunday.
  • Direction and meaning are separate: the arrow shows whether the number went up or down, the color shows whether that is good or bad for that metric.
  • Branch rows open a detail with the same figures, so the totals at the top can be traced down to each branch.
