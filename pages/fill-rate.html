About this screen

  • Each order line is matched with the invoice that came from that order, so the fill rate is measured line by line, not by comparing monthly totals.
  • Fill rate by lines counts lines invoiced complete; fill rate by value weighs each line by its amount. Both are needed: many small misses hurt service, a few big ones hurt revenue.
  • Every missing unit gets a reason: no stock, cancelled by the customer or a price change. Only "no stock" is a purchasing problem.
  • The cards, the summary, the chart and the table footer all follow the same filters.
