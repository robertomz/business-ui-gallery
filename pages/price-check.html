About this screen

  • Margin is calculated over the sale price: (price − cost) / price. A 25% margin means price = cost / (1 − 0.25), not cost × 1.25.
  • Cost change compares the last purchase cost with the cost when the price was last changed, so a supplier increase shows up before it eats the margin.
  • Nobody changes a price list directly: each change becomes a request with its reason, and the approver sees the margin before and after.
  • Percentages are stored as fractions (0.25) and only formatted as 25.0% on screen.
