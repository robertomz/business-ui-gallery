About this screen

  • Each asset has a maintenance plan with a frequency. Completing a job schedules the next one automatically from the day it was really done, not from the planned day.
  • A job can only be closed when its checklist is complete, or with a note that explains what was left pending.
  • Overdue jobs stay visible on every screen until someone completes or reschedules them.
