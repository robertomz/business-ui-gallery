About this screen

  • The due date is the document date plus the payee's credit days, so treasury sees what is coming before it is due.
  • Expense accounts (code 6) only appear enabled when the selected cost center has budget for them. The rest stay visible but disabled, with the reason.
  • Lines marked non-deductible are still paid, but they are reported apart for taxes.
  • Every request starts as pending. Rejecting one requires a reason, which goes back to the person who asked.
