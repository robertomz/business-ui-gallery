About this screen

  • Each seller's goal is the sum of their customers' monthly goals, so the drill-down always adds up to the seller row.
  • Expected to date = goal × working days elapsed ÷ working days in the month. Sundays and holidays are excluded on both sides.
  • Projection extends the current daily average over the working days left; it is a reference, not a forecast model.
  • Status always shows an icon and a word, not only a color, and the totals follow the filters.
