About this screen

  • The same numbers can be read by account or by center, and as budget, actual or variance, without leaving the page.
  • The first column and the header stay in place while scrolling through twelve months.
  • Every figure opens the documents that make it up, so a variance can be explained on the spot.
  • Variances carry a sign and an arrow, not only a color.
