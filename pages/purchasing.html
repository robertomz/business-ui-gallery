About this screen

  • Days of inventory = stock ÷ average daily sales of the last 30 days.
  • Suggested quantity = average daily sales × (lead time + safety days) − stock − units in transit, rounded up to full cases. Safety days = lead time × safety %.
  • Status uses icon and text as well as color: Shortage (stock lasts less than the lead time), Reorder (below lead time + safety), OK, Overstock (above the day limit) and No movement.
  • Selected lines become one purchase order per supplier; quantities can still be adjusted before confirming.
