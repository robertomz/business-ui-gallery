About this screen

  • The devices only record punches; the schedule decides what each punch means. A 10-minute grace period applies before a late arrival counts.
  • Overtime counts only after 30 extra minutes and in half-hour blocks, the way payroll pays it.
  • A day with only one punch is a missing punch: it cannot be paid until someone corrects it, so the cut-off is blocked while any remain.
  • Closing the cut-off locks the week and sends overtime hours and absences to the payroll run; after that, corrections go through a payroll adjustment.
