Employee file

One record per employee: contract, position, vacations, documents and every change made to it.

About this screen

  • The real record has dozens of fields; here they are grouped in tabs so the person only sees what the task needs.
  • Department and position are cascading: the positions offered are only those of the chosen department, and a new one can be created without leaving the form.
  • Fixed-term contracts show their extensions in order, and documents warn 30 days before they expire.
  • Every save writes a line to the History tab with who changed what, from which value to which.
