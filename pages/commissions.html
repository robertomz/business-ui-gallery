About this screen

  • Tiers are validated as a whole: they must start at 0%, connect without gaps and never overlap, and every error appears on the row that causes it.
  • The commission has two parts: sales against the goal and collection of the seller's receivables, so selling on credit that never gets collected pays less.
  • Closing the month recalculates everything on the server side in the real system and freezes the tiers used, so later changes to the tiers never rewrite what was paid.

Close month

After closing, the tiers and results of this month can no longer be edited.
